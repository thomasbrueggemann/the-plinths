03 / About

The Band

© CAPSULA.DK

The Plinths are an emerging alternative rock quartet ready to make their mark on the Swedish music scene — based in Lund, this international band creates music that cuts through modern noise with straightforward energy and honest songwriting.

Their songs are raw but catchy, direct but thoughtful. Fresh off recording their debut EP "Alive in the Hive" live at Tambourine Studios in Malmö, The Plinths have just released their first single "Hive" and are eager to bring their sound to venues across Sweden and beyond.

The band writes about emotional honesty, social isolation, and the difficulty of expressing yourself without judgment.
